Make it sound like YOUR keyboard
  1. Open the voice recorder (or camera) on your phone.
  2. Put the phone right next to your keyboard, in a quiet room.
  3. Press about 30 different keys, one at a time, slowly (about 2 per second). Hit some hard and some soft. Press the space bar a few times at the end.
  4. Send the recording to this device and choose it below.
